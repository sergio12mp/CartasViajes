# Legal content review

Internal working notes. The public pages summarize the current service; they are not a completed compliance review. Keep `legalContentReviewed` false until the outstanding decisions have been resolved and the published text reflects them.

## Outstanding decisions

- Confirm the legal basis for internal usage and sharing metrics and document any assessment required. Do not assume consent or legitimate interest from the presence of a feature.
- Define retention periods or criteria for accounts, games, submissions, usage records and purchases. Confirm how deletion requests and any mandatory retention are handled before promising a deadline or automatic deletion.
- Confirm the contracted entities, regions and transfer safeguards for Google, Vercel, Neon, Stripe and optional notification/video services. The operator's country does not establish where providers process data.
- Review operator identification requirements against the actual activity of the service. Only publish confirmed information; do not derive personal details from credentials.
- Before commercializing packs, complete applicable fiscal and seller information, access duration and scope, incident handling, withdrawal and refund terms. The terms page reflects the existing payment flag; it does not disable purchases or provide completed sale terms.
- Confirm the bases for optional features and community submissions, the information shown at collection, and whether additional information is needed for names provided by trip creators.

## Editorial boundaries

- Keep account data, purposes, visibility, providers, rights and actual retention behavior understandable in the public privacy policy.
- Keep the outstanding privacy points visible until resolved. Moving working notes here must not imply that missing information has been completed.
- Keep cookie purposes and lifetimes available in the expandable details. Verify them against the deployed authentication configuration when it changes.
- Game instructions belong in the FAQ and in the play interface. Terms retain participation, voluntary challenges, shared-content rules and a link to the game rules.
- Existing purchase data may remain even when payments are disabled. Do not remove relevant privacy information solely because the current beta has no checkout.
